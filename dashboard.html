🌱 Seed Node — one network for everyone

Your laptop is the seed — the heart of the network. Anyone who joins with your seed address mines the same chain as you. Without it, they'd mine a lonely separate chain.

  1. Open seed.txt (next to the miner file) and write your seed address on the first line, e.g. 203.0.113.45:9333
  2. Same WiFi friends: use your laptop's local IP (type ipconfig in cmd → IPv4 Address).
  3. Far-away friends: use your public IP + forward port 9333 in your router.
  4. Send them the miner zip — their laptop joins your network automatically. ✅
Scaling note: one home laptop can't serve thousands of friends. For a big launch, a small 24/7 cloud server becomes the public seed — same seed.txt, just a new address.
