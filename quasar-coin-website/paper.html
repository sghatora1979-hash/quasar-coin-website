Quasar Coin
Technical Paper

How Quasar works: mining, coin creation, halving, distribution, and the road ahead. Written by the Quasar team — our design, our concept.

Version 1.014 October 2026© 2026 Quasar Coin

1The Concept

Quasar is an independent digital-coin system designed from scratch by our team: a mining-based coin with a fixed supply, a transparent three-phase emission schedule, and a short, honest founder vesting. No premine trading, no hidden issuance — every rule below is enforced by the software itself.

30-second blocks

A new block is created roughly every 30 seconds, day and night.

500B hard cap

No more than 500 billion QSR can ever exist. The limit is in the code.

Laptop mining

Designed so an ordinary laptop can mine. No special hardware required to start.

1-week vesting

The founder allocation unlocks over one week — then it is fully in circulation logic.

2How Mining Works

Mining is a fair lottery run by mathematics. Every computer on the network competes to solve a cryptographic puzzle; the winner's block is added to the chain and new coins are created as its reward.

The puzzle, in plain words. Each block's data is hashed (SHA-256). The hash must begin with a set number of zeros. There is no shortcut — the only way is to try nonces one by one until a valid hash appears. A faster computer tries more per second, so it wins more often — but the reward per block never changes. More miners do not create more coins; they share the same fixed schedule.

3How Coins Are Created

New QSR is created only as mining rewards, on a fixed three-phase schedule. The schedule is a pure function of block height — it cannot be changed by any miner, including the founder.

4Halving — How and When

Halving is triggered by coins mined (block height), never by calendar date. When block 6,000,000 arrives, the reward halves to 25,000; at block 8,629,800 it halves again to 12,500. Because block time is held at ~30 seconds by difficulty adjustment, the phases last ~5.7, 2.5 and 2.5 years — but if mining ever slowed, halving would simply wait for the coins. Joining miners cannot shorten a phase; they only share its fixed rewards.

No hidden issuance. There is no function anywhere in the Quasar software that creates coins outside (a) the mining schedule above or (b) the single disclosed founder allocation. Every node independently validates every block reward and every founder claim, and rejects anything beyond the schedule.

6Founder Allocation & Vesting

100 billion QSR (20% of the cap) is reserved as a transparent founder allocation. It is not mined and no founder reward is paid on mining blocks. It unlocks linearly over one week (20,160 blocks at 30 seconds each) and can only be claimed manually, up to the unlocked amount. After one week the full allocation is unlocked.

7The Network

  • Peer-to-peer. Every computer runs the same software and shares blocks directly (port 9333). There is no central server that can be switched off.
  • Every node validates everything. Block hashes, proof-of-work, reward amounts, founder claims and signatures are re-checked by each node.
  • Difficulty self-adjusts. Every 10 blocks the network retargets difficulty toward the 30-second pace — the schedule's timing holds no matter how many miners join.
  • Wallets. Real cryptographic keypairs; addresses begin with Q. Your private key never leaves your device.

8Using QSR

QSR lives in wallets and moves address-to-address with signed transactions — no bank in between. The practical economy is being built in stages:

  • Today: mine on a laptop, hold in the wallet, transfer to any Q address.
  • Next: verified sellers accept mined QSR for real gadgets, ordered over WhatsApp/Telegram — buyer sends QSR, seller ships.
  • Later: exchange listing, so QSR can trade on the open market.

9Community & Growth Plan

A coin is only as strong as its community. The rollout plan:

  • Phase 1 — now: the founder's node mines and secures the chain; the website onboards the first users.
  • Phase 2 — Oct–Dec: 10–15 community laptops join mining; sellers are verified for the gadget program.
  • Phase 3: public launch with one-click mining; referrals and community rewards grow the network.
  • Phase 4: exchange listing.

10Value Design

Quasar's economics are designed around scarcity and utility:

  • Fixed cap. At most 500B QSR can ever exist; actual issuance is 498.6B.
  • Decreasing new supply. Halvings cut new coins per block from 50,000 to 25,000 to 12,500 — then to zero.
  • Growing utility. As more people mine, hold, and spend QSR (gadgets, transfers), demand is driven by real use.
Honest note. These are design mechanics, not promises. QSR currently has no market price and no monetary value. Never buy, mine, or hold any coin based on expected price gains — only on your own judgment of its utility and design.
